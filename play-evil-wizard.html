Choose from 15 champions and fight through an interconnected dark-fantasy campaign. Click inside the game before using keyboard, mouse, or controller input.

Desktop: W/A/S/D move, E or W jump, Q crouch, Space or left mouse attack, Shift/X dash, L/I abilities, U ultimate, F interact, and backquote opens the control console. On phones and tablets, rotate to landscape and use the left stick, square action buttons, and console button.
