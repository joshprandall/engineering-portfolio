# Background field recordings

The Day MP3 copies are conversions of the original OGG recordings listed below. Both formats are preserved locally with the site, and remain subject to their original terms.

- River: “Sanna river rapids,” Jarrod stanley / J. D. Savanyu. CC0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sanna_river_rapids.ogg
- Waterfall: “Water fall,” Benzband. Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported. The MP3 conversion retains this license; no content was added to the recording. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Water_fall.ogg License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- Beach near: “Cape May Shorebirds closer,” U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service. Public domain as a U.S. federal employee's official work. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cape_May_Shorebirds_closer.ogg
- Beach far: “Cape May Shorebirds (distant),” U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service. Public domain as a U.S. federal employee's official work. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cape_May_Shorebirds_(distant).ogg

The existing Night soundtrack is user-provided and unchanged. No new ownership or licensing claim is made for it.

See ../scenes/CREDITS.md for the unchanged video and Night-image attributions. The local Day posters are frames extracted from those same videos. The river video has been remuxed for earlier playback initialization; its encoded video packets are unchanged.
