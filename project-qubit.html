QUANTUM / EDUCATIONAL MODEL

One qubit. Two outcomes.

Explore the probabilities of measuring a single qubit along one meridian of the Bloch sphere. This is an interactive classical simulation—not quantum hardware and not a two-qubit entanglement model.

Open the interactive qubit lab ↗︎Source on GitHub ↗︎

What you can test

Change the polar angle θ and compare P(0) = cos²(θ/2) with P(1) = sin²(θ/2). Explore the limits θ = 0°, 90° and 180° and repeat measurements to see sampling variability.

The restricted model fixes the relative phase at zero and does not simulate a device-specific noise model. Use the separate entanglement visualization on the homepage for a different two-qubit concept.
