03 / BOUNDARIES

What this model does—and does not—show.

It does show

  • A normalized pure single-qubit state with arbitrary relative phase.
  • How θ and φ map to Bloch-vector coordinates.
  • Z, X, and Y basis probabilities from the Born rule.
  • Random sampling from those probabilities.

It does not show

  • Mixed states or density-matrix dynamics.
  • Multi-qubit entanglement.
  • Noise, decoherence, gates, or hardware calibration.
  • A connection to a quantum processor.

This is a classical browser simulation for learning and visualization. It is not a quantum-hardware emulator.
