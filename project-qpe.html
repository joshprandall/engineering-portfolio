Quantum Phase Estimation

What the experiment means

The input is an eigenstate of U: applying U multiplies it by exp(2πiφ). Controlled powers of U imprint that phase on the counting register. The inverse quantum Fourier transform converts the relative phases into a distribution over integers y, giving the estimate y / 2ⁿ.

For φ = 1/8 and three counting qubits, the ideal output is 001 with probability 1. Between exactly representable phases, nearby outcomes share probability. This simulation assumes a perfect eigenstate and exact controlled operations.

Uniform mixing combines the ideal distribution with a uniform distribution. It is an illustrative noise model, not a device-specific hardware prediction.
