# Website 2.0 destinations

Primary destinations: Home, About Me, Expertise & Experience, Résumé, Direction, Projects, Learn, Game Development and AI Development. Existing URLs remain; Home's former missing `#about` action now targets its dedicated page. The original homepage content is retained, with an added section-navigation solar.

Learn's separate solar derives seven domains from the real corpus: Cloud & Systems, Advanced Computing, Software Engineering, Cybersecurity, Leadership & IT Management, Mathematics and Physics. Each dedicated `learn-<domain>.html` page offers available progression/activity filters, a locally saved chosen-lesson plan, dedicated lesson links and routes to labs, practice, mastery and evidence. It does not invent unsupported research depth or mark a chosen plan as completed learning.

AI Development supplies nineteen approach/project paths with foundations, minimal builds, advanced extensions and limitations to evaluate. Existing projects and corpus searches are linked; proposed implementations are clearly future work. Game Development retains both working games and adds the future engine and discipline paths. Engine design source lives under `project-sources/custom-engine/`, excluded from production; its public explanation is `engine-development.html`.

Controllers and their routes are shipped as one complete allowlisted package. No shared-controller-only deployment is supported.
