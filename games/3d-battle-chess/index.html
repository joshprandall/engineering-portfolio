How to play

Tap or click a piece, then a highlighted square. Desktop players can use Arrow keys or W/A/S/D to move the board cursor and Enter/Space to select. A controller can use the left stick/D-pad and A/B/X/Y buttons. V switches 2D/3D, F flips the board, U undoes, M toggles sound, and N starts a new game.

All normal chess rules are enforced, including check, checkmate, castling, en passant, promotion, stalemate, repetition, and the fifty-move rule.
