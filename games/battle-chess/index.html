Castling, en passant, promotion, check, and checkmate follow standard chess moves. A king is never captured—the game ends at checkmate.

This casual version ends draws automatically on threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule, stalemate, or the engine’s insufficient-material detection. It has no tournament clocks or draw-claim procedure.
