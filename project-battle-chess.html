Crown & Ash — 3D Battle Chess

A local two-player chess game with themed 3D armies, legal move enforcement, tactical fallback controls, and animated captures.

← Back to all projects
Play the working game ↗View source on GitHub ↗

Included

Seven themed sets, legal chess rules, castling, en passant, promotion, local saves, JSON match export/import, camera controls, capture choreography, optional sound, reduced motion, and an accessible tactical board when WebGL is unavailable.

This is a playable local two-player release. It does not claim online matchmaking, accounts, a computer opponent, or a backend.
