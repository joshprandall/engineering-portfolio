JOSHUA RANDALL KNOWLEDGE PLATFORM — v0.3.0 RELEASE NOTES
2026-09-06

CONTENT
- 184 published learning objects (+72 from v0.2.0)
- 24 guided learning paths
- 122 searchable glossary terms
- 316 mapped curriculum topics awaiting publication
- 7 major domains

This release adds a large cross-domain content pack focused on practical,
connected explanations rather than isolated trivia. It includes new labs,
troubleshooting guides, foundational lessons, and intermediate systems material.

NAVIGATION / EXPERIENCE
The existing v0.2.x platform capabilities remain intact: weighted full-text
search, filters, curriculum explorer, knowledge graph, bookmarks, local progress,
continue-learning state, lesson narration and audio summaries, reduced-motion
support, quizzes, references, related lessons, glossary search, random lesson,
and interactive visual demonstrations.

DEPLOYMENT
Copy the CONTENTS of public_html into the existing OSU public_html and replace
matching files. Do not create public_html/public_html.
